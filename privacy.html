Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Imperial Fiesta ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you register for our event.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us through the registration form, including:

  • Personal Details: Your name, email address, branch, and mobile number.
  • Event Preferences: Your hobbies, game suggestions, and your interest in participating in activities.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To manage your registration and payment for the event.
  • To communicate with you about event details, updates, and announcements.
  • To plan and organize event activities based on the preferences and suggestions provided.
  • To identify participants for various performances and games.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Your data is stored on a secure server and is only accessible to authorized event organizers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information may be shared internally among the organizing committee solely for the purpose of organizing the event.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the details provided on our Contact page.
