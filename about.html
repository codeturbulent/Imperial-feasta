About Imperial Fiesta

Our Mission

Imperial Fiesta is more than just an event; it's a tradition. Our mission is to create a warm, welcoming, and unforgettable experience for all incoming junior students. We aim to bridge the gap between seniors and juniors, fostering a community of friendship, collaboration, and mutual respect right from the start of the academic journey.

What We Do

Every year, the senior batch organizes this grand celebration filled with music, dance, games, and delightful performances. It's a day to break the ice, showcase hidden talents, and make memories that will last a lifetime. We believe in creating an inclusive environment where every student feels like a part of the family.

The Organizing Team

The event is brought to you by a dedicated team of senior students who are passionate about upholding the spirit of our institution. We work tirelessly to ensure every detail is perfect, from the decorations to the entertainment. Meet the core coordinators for this year's event:

  • Ashish Goyal: Event Head & Logistics
  • Aryan Sengar: Creative & Performance Lead
  • And a fantastic team of volunteers from the senior batch!
