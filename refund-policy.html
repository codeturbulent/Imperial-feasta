Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 16, 2025

1. General Policy

All tickets purchased for the Imperial Fiesta 2025 are non-refundable. As a student-organized event, the funds collected from registrations are immediately used to pay for deposits and book services such as venue arrangements, and the DJ. This commitment of funds means we are unable to process refunds once a ticket has been purchased.

2. Event Cancellation

Approved Refund amount will be credited via original payment method within 10 days

3. Ticket Transferability

While we cannot offer refunds, you may be able to transfer your ticket to another junior student from our university who has not yet registered. To do so, you must contact the event organizers with the full details of the person you wish to transfer the ticket to. All transfers must be officially confirmed by the organizing committee to be valid. Please reach out to us via the details on our Contact Us page at least 48 hours before the event to request a transfer.

4. No-Show Policy

No refunds will be issued for attendees who do not show up to the event.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this refund policy, please do not hesitate to get in touch with us. We appreciate your understanding and support in making this event a grand success.
